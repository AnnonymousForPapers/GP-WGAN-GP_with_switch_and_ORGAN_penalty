Final training datasets used in the manuscript

Bladder cancer dataset:
  File: Bladder.4.0_test_mut.csv
  Number of peptides: 6,232

Brain cancer dataset:
  File: Brain.4.0_test_mut.csv
  Number of peptides: 2,454

The peptide sequences were obtained from TSNAdb:
http://biopharm.zju.edu.cn/tsnadb/

Reference:
Wu, Jingcheng, et al. "TSNAdb: a database for tumor-specific neoantigens
from immunogenomics data analysis." Genomics, Proteomics & Bioinformatics
16.4 (2018): 276-282.

The datasets contain 9- and 10-mer cancer-associated neoantigen sequences
used for model training. The peptides were selected for HLA-A*02:01 with
predicted binding affinity <500 nM using NetMHCpan 4.0.

The HLA allele is stored as "HLA-A*0201" in the CSV files, which represents
HLA-A*02:01.
